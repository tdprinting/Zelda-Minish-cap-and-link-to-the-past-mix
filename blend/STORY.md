# The Picori Seal: the story, and where each A Link to the Past item is found

## The legend so far

Long ago a thief of shadow seized the Golden Power. The Picori brought a hero a sacred
sword and a golden light, and the hero sealed the shadow, **Ganon**, in a Dark World.
Seven sages kept the seal. Now **Vaati** has returned, and his servant **Agahnim** feeds
on Ganon's power: the sages' seven maidens are held in the Dark World's palaces, and if
the seal fails, the shadow comes back.

## The Knights' Relics (planned, not built yet)

This section is a design for a later version. None of it is in the game yet; v1.2's item
places are listed in README.md.

The hero did not fight alone. The **Knights of Hyrule** stood beside the sages, and the
tools they fought with, the relics that let a hero cross between the worlds, read the
old tongue and break the shadow's walls, were too dangerous to leave lying about: what
helped seal a shadow could also free one. So when the war was over the knights hid
every relic where only the next hero could claim it, each in a place of its own:
in tombs and caves of the Light World, behind tablets carved in old Hylian, and in the
palaces of the Dark World itself, where the knights who guarded the seal from the inside
kept watch until the end. Agahnim hunts the relics now, to break the seal for good.

**Sahasrahla**, the last elder of the knights' line, lives in his hut in the Eastern
Hills. He tells Link the legend the first time they meet, and every time Link comes
back he says where the next relic waits. Each relic's place has its own keeper or
inscription that tells its part of the story.
