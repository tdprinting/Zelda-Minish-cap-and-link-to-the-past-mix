# Zelda: The Picori Seal (Minish Cap × A Link to the Past)

The blended game is built on the Minish Cap decompilation in [`../tmc`](../tmc), with
A Link to the Past systems and content brought over from [`../zelda3`](../zelda3).
Everything is made by restyling and reusing the art already in Minish Cap.

## What's in it so far

| Feature | From | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark World | A Link to the Past | Every overworld area has a Dark World version. Its palettes are restyled at runtime (olive grass, brown earth, violet shadows), so no new art is needed. |
| Magic Mirror | A Link to the Past | Comes with the Fire Element from Mt. Crenel (this game's Death Mountain). Then hold **L + R** on the overworld to cross between the Light and Dark World. The room reloads in the other world, and the current world is saved with your file. |
| Moon Pearl | A Link to the Past | Without it Link is a pink bunny in the Dark World and can't use his sword or items. It comes with the Ocarina after the Fortress of Winds, the third dungeon, like the Tower of Hera. |
| Fire Rod and Ice Rod | A Link to the Past | Minish Cap's unused Fire Rod, finished, lies in a clearing of the Dark World Minish Woods (Skull Woods). The Ice Rod waits on Lake Hylia's stump island for anyone with Flippers. Link swings them like the Pacci Cane: one shoots a red fireball, the other a white ice bolt. They share the cane's menu slot, and **L + A** or **L + B** cycles the rod on that button. |
| Bombos, Ether, Quake | A Link to the Past | Medallions found in the Dark World: Bombos buried in the ruins (dig with the Mole Mitts), Ether on the Mt. Crenel summit, Quake on the Lake Hylia stump island. **L + Select** picks one, **R + Select** casts it. A cast needs a sword, works once per room and strikes every enemy on screen. |
| Hookshot, Hammer, Canes of Byrna and Somaria, Magic Powder | A Link to the Past | Each one shares a menu slot with a Minish Cap item, since the menu has no room left: Hookshot with the Gust Jar, Hammer with the Mole Mitts, Byrna with the Shield, Powder with the Lantern, Somaria with the Bombs. Once you own both, **L + the item's button** switches that slot. The Hookshot grabs walls and pulls Link across, the Hammer smashes and stuns, Byrna wraps Link in a damaging, protective ring, Powder turns weak enemies into fairies, and Somaria makes a pushable block (one per room). Where they are: Hookshot in the Dark World Castor Wilds (Swamp Palace), Hammer in the dark Eastern Hills (Palace of Darkness), Byrna buried on the dark Mt. Crenel, Powder in the Light World Minish Woods once you have the Lantern, Somaria at the dark Veil Falls (Misery Mire). |
| Book of Mudora, Magic Cape, Bug Net, Mushroom, Shovel, Titan's Mitt | A Link to the Past | The Book lies in Hyrule Town's library; the Desert Palace stays shut without it, and the Bombos and Ether tablets need it to be read. The Magic Cape lies under the Royal Valley graves: Link vanishes and can't be hurt for ten seconds, or until he uses it again. The Bug Net is in the schoolroom once Link has a bottle, and catches a fairy into an empty bottle (with none free, the fairy heals him). The Mushroom grows in the Minish Woods where the Powder used to lie, and Syrup the witch brews it into Magic Powder. The Shovel is at Dampe's house and digs soft ground like the Mole Mitts, sometimes turning up a Rupee or a heart. The Titan's Mitt is Thieves' Town's big chest prize, opens the way to Misery Mire, and replaces the Power Bracelets on the Quest Status page. |
| A Link to the Past items page | New | **Select** on the pause menu's Items page switches to a second page with every A Link to the Past item and its own icon and name. A or B puts the chosen item on that button: the medallions and the Mirror are used straight from it too, and a shared-slot item switches its slot over (choosing the Minish Cap item on the first page switches it back). |
| Eastern Palace | A Link to the Past | The first A Link to the Past dungeon, room for room: its walls, pits, doors, stairs, chests and enemies come from the original layout. It is drawn in a blended, cel-shaded style: A Link to the Past's own walls and floors, relit in Minish Cap's warmer colors, flattened into cartoon color bands with bold brown-black outlines, bright rim light on edges and soft shadows cast across the floor, calm stone floors, dark space around the rooms, plus Minish Cap's chests and doors. Space A Link to the Past never let you see is walled off. The cracked cave in the Eastern Hills opens once Link has the Earth Element. Inside are the map, compass, a small key, the Big Key and the Bow in the big chest, key doors, a basement, and the Armos Knights; beating them brings out a Heart Container. |
| Desert Palace | A Link to the Past | The second A Link to the Past dungeon, built the same way, in area 0x1B. A cracked cave in the Trilby Highlands opens once Link has the Fire Element (when the Magic Mirror comes) and the Book of Mudora to read the words at its door. Inside: the map, compass, Big Key, small keys from the dash statue and under pots, the Power Bracelets in the big chest (A Link to the Past's Power Glove; Minish Cap's bracelets do the same lifting), the east wing's door leading round to the back entrance, a locked staircase, and the Lanmolas, who leave a Heart Container. |
| Tower of Hera | A Link to the Past | The third A Link to the Past dungeon, in area 0x1C, up six floors of stairs. A cracked cave high on Mt. Crenel opens once Link has the Ocarina (when the Moon Pearl comes, as in A Link to the Past after its third dungeon). Its big chest holds the Mirror Shield, since the Moon Pearl already comes with the Ocarina. Moldorm waits at the top with a Heart Container. Its crystal switches work: a sword hit flips the blue and orange pegs in every room of the tower. |
| Palace of Darkness | A Link to the Past | The first Dark World dungeon. The Eastern Hills cave leads here instead of the Eastern Palace when Link is in the Dark World with the Moon Pearl. The big chest holds a larger quiver; the Helmasaur King waits at the end, and only the Hammer cracks his mask. |
| Swamp Palace | A Link to the Past | Through the Darknut cave of the Dark World Castor Wilds, with the Moon Pearl and Flippers. The big chest holds a bigger Bomb Bag; Arrghus is guarded by a ring of Arrghi that the Hookshot pulls away. |
| Skull Woods | A Link to the Past | Through the Great Fairy's tree in the Dark World Minish Woods, with the Moon Pearl and the Fire Rod. The big chest holds a bigger wallet; a hole in the Gibdo hall drops into Mothula's lair, where spike blocks slide while she flies and fires beams. |
| Thieves' Town | A Link to the Past | In area 0x39. In the Dark World, with the Moon Pearl and the Hammer, the stranger's house in Hyrule Town leads here (A Link to the Past's Village of Outcasts). The hub's four quarters, joined in A Link to the Past by raised walkways, are joined by gaps in their walls. The big chest holds the Titan's Mitt, which opens the way to Misery Mire. Blind's door takes the Big Key; his room gives a Heart Container. |
| Ice Palace | A Link to the Past | In area 0x3A, through the cave on Lake Hylia's south shore, in the Dark World, with the Moon Pearl, the Flippers and the Fire Rod. Link drops through the floors as in A Link to the Past: the cracked floor of the first octagon, a hole into the big chest's room, and the floor below the Big Key door into Kholdstare's lair. The big chest holds a bigger Bomb Bag, since Minish Cap has no armor. |
| Misery Mire | A Link to the Past | In area 0x3B, through the cave at the foot of the dark Veil Falls. As in A Link to the Past, it opens once Link casts Ether in front of it, and stays open; he also needs the Titan's Mitt from Thieves' Town to go in. A bridge crosses the gap past the entrance. The big chest holds a bigger wallet, since the Cane of Somaria already lies at the dark Veil Falls. Vitreous's room gives a Heart Container. |
| Turtle Rock | A Link to the Past | In area 0x3C, through the cave on Mt. Crenel's summit. It opens once Link casts Quake there, and he needs the Cane of Somaria to go in. Somaria's tracks and the pipes over the pits are laid as bridges, since Minish Cap has no platform to ride. The big chest holds a bigger quiver, since the Mirror Shield is the Tower of Hera's. Trinexx's room gives a Heart Container. |
| Ganon's Tower | A Link to the Past | In area 0x3D. It stands where the Tower of Hera does, as in A Link to the Past: in the Dark World, Hera's cracked cave on Mt. Crenel stays shut until Link has the seven crystals, that is until he has beaten the bosses of all seven Dark World dungeons. Inside is the tower's main way up, 21 of its rooms: the Armos Knights and the Big Key in the basement, the Big Key door, the Lanmolas and Moldorm again, and Agahnim's hall at the top (beating Agahnim there gives a Heart Container). Bridges and steps take the places A Link to the Past reaches by Hookshot or over a ledge. The big chest holds a piece of heart, since Minish Cap has no armor. |
| Pyramid of Power | A Link to the Past | Ganon's hall (room 0x00), in area 0x3E. In A Link to the Past Link falls into it through the hole Ganon smashes in the Pyramid after Agahnim's second defeat; here, in the Dark World, the Castle Garden's castle door leads down into it once Agahnim has fallen atop Ganon's Tower (in the Light World, and before that, it is still Hyrule Castle's door). Link lands where A Link to the Past puts him; steps in the hall's corner lead back up to the garden. The room goes dark in Ganon's last phase, and his two torches stand on the hall's own. Beating Ganon ends the game: the victory tune, a fade to white, and the credits. |
| Link's item animations | A Link to the Past | Every A Link to the Past item has its own Link animation, drawn from that game's item art in Link's Minish Cap colors: the Hammer is lifted and slammed, the rods and Somaria are swung and pointed, the Hookshot is held out, Byrna is raised, the Powder bag is shaken, the medallions and Mirror are held up high, the Bug Net is swung, the Shovel is driven down, and the Magic Cape is flung up and swept around. |
| A Link to the Past enemies | A Link to the Past | Stalfos (some drop from the ceiling and hop away from sword swings), green and red Eyegores that only open their eye to chase, Popos, Anti-fairies, and the Armos Knights: six knights hop in formation, then the last turns red and slams down. In the Desert Palace: Leevers that burrow and spin up, Beamos statues that sweep and fire lasers, and the Lanmolas bursting out of the sand. In the Tower of Hera: Hardhat Beetles that skate and get knocked back, Bumpers, and the Moldorm boss, which can only be hurt in the tail. The Dark World dungeons add Terrorpins that only the Hammer flips, Mini Helmasaurs, Hovers, Kyamerons, Zols, Firesnakes and wall shooters; the Desert and Tower get Debirandos, Kodongos and small Moldorms. Hinoxes throw bombs in the Dark World hills, and Pokeys, Gibdos and Baris are built too. The last four Dark World dungeons bring Blind (who loses his head three times), Kholdstare in its ice shell (Fire Rod or Bombos), Vitreous and its thirteen eyes, and Trinexx (Ice Rod on the red head, Fire Rod on the blue, then the snake), plus Freezors, Pengators, Stalfos Knights, Wizzrobes, Laser Eyes, Chain Chomps, Zazaks, Gibos, fire bars and Stalfos heads. Agahnim waits at the top of Ganon's Tower with his two shadow copies: bat his energy balls back with the sword. Ganon waits in the Pyramid of Power: his trident, fire bats, then the dark room, where the Fire Rod or the lantern lights his two torches and only silver arrows (the Light Arrow bow) finish him. Bosses start and fight fully on screen, and Blind first appears as the maiden. They use A Link to the Past's own sprites, with new frames added. |
| Dark World enemies | Both | In the Dark World, Octoroks become A Link to the Past's Ropes, which coil and lunge, and Spear Moblins become its Moblins, which throw spears. ChuChus become Spiny ChuChus and Beetles become Spiked Beetles. |
| Blended overworld | Both | The overworld's colors move toward A Link to the Past's Light World (deeper greens, golden dirt, blue water) in the same cel-shaded style as the dungeons, and trees, cliffs, walls and buildings cast soft shadows. |
| Blended story | Both | The opening legend tells of the Golden Power, the shadow sealed in a Dark World and the seven sages. Ezlo explains Ganon, Vaati and Agahnim the first time Link reaches the Dark World, and the castle and final boss text name Ganon's shadow. |
| Split-screen co-op | New | Two copies of the game run side by side in one window. Each player sees the other as a second Link in red mail when they are in the same room and world, including sword swings. The partner's sword hits enemies on your screen too. |
| Going down and reviving | New | Each player has their own hearts, items and Rupees. A player who runs out of hearts lies down instead of getting a game over, and the partner sees a fairy over them. Standing next to them and pressing R uses a **Revive Fairy**, which brings them back with half their hearts. The game is over only when both are down, or when the downed player holds A + B to give up. |
| Revive Fairy | New | A new item with its own art (a corked jar with a heart lid and a pink fairy). Stockwell sells it for 60 Rupees on the crates by his counter. Each player carries their own, up to nine. |
| Quick inventory | New | Each player can open their own item menu over their half of the screen (Q, Numpad 0 or a gamepad's Y). It shows every item and the Revive Fairies; A or B puts the chosen item on that button. Only that player's game slows to half speed while it is open. "Go to Player N" warps you to your partner. |
| Shared progress | New | Chests, keys, doors, bosses, story events, heart containers and pieces, wallet and bag upgrades, and the equipment and key items one player finds reach the partner's game too. Hearts, Rupees, bombs, arrows, bottle contents and Revive Fairies stay each player's own. |
| Carry and throw your partner | New | Walk up to your partner and press R to lift them overhead, then R or A to throw them. The carried player can still swing their sword, so one player can walk while the other fights. Thrown players land on their feet. |
| Sharing | New | Standing close, hold L and press R to give your partner one of your hearts (you always keep one). In the quick inventory, L on Bombs, Arrows or the Revive Fairies sends some to your partner. |
| Co-op puzzles | New | Puzzles that need both players, with new art: **Twin Stones** must be struck by sword within a second of each other, and they stand too far apart for one player; a **Toss Bell** rings only when one player throws the other into it. South Hyrule Field (two stones, reward: a Revive Fairy), Hyrule Town's south square (a bell, reward: 100 Rupees) and Castle Garden (two stones in the hedge maze and a bell, reward: a Revive Fairy). They only appear in co-op, and each player gets the reward. |

## Playing on Windows

1. Build `zelda-blend.gba` from your own Minish Cap (USA) ROM (below).
2. Put `zelda-blend.exe` and `zelda-blend.gba` in the same folder and run the exe.

| Player 1 (keyboard) | Player 2 (keyboard) | GBA button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W A S D | Arrow keys | D-pad |
| K | Numpad 2 | A |
| J | Numpad 1 | B |
| U / I | Numpad 4 / 5 | L / R |
| Enter | Numpad Enter | Start |
| Right Shift | Numpad + | Select |
| Q | Numpad 0 | Quick inventory (gamepad: Y) |

To revive a fallen partner, stand next to them and press R. You need a Revive
Fairy from Stockwell's shop. A player who is down can hold A + B to give up.
Next to your partner, R lifts them (R or A throws) and L + R gives them a heart.
In the quick inventory, L gives the selected Bombs, Arrows or a Revive Fairy.

Gamepads work too: the first controller is player 1, the second is player 2.
**F1** switches between side-by-side and stacked screens, **F11** toggles fullscreen,
**Esc** quits. Each player has their own save (`player1.sav`, `player2.sav`).
Run `zelda-blend.exe --single` to play alone.

The ROM also runs in any GBA emulator for single-player.

### Updates

The game updates itself. When it starts, it looks for a newer `picori-seal-vX.Y.zip`
in your Downloads folder and in an `updates` folder next to the game, and asks
whether to install it. Installing swaps in the new game and program, keeps your
saves, puts the old version (and a copy of the saves) in a `previous` folder, and
restarts the game. So to update, download the new zip and start the game.

Builds contain your ROM, so they never come from a public server. To have the game
fetch new versions by itself, add a private place to `update.ini` (made next to the
game on first start): a folder your cloud drive syncs, the URL of a `latest.json`
manifest, or `github:you/your-private-repo` with a read-only `token`, for the latest
release of a private GitHub repo. `check = 0` turns checks off, `ask = 0` installs
without asking, and `--no-update` skips the check once.

## Building

ROMs and anything built from them stay on your machine; `.gitignore` blocks them.

```sh
# The game ROM (Linux or WSL; toolchain in tmc/INSTALL.md)
./blend/build.sh path/to/minish-cap-usa.gba path/to/alttp-usa.sfc   # -> blend/out/zelda-blend.gba

# The co-op player, Linux
make -C blend/host                               # needs libsdl2-dev, libmgba-dev

# The co-op player, Windows (cross-compiled; see the script for its dependencies)
./blend/host/build-windows.sh                    # -> blend/out/zelda-blend.exe

# A release zip and its latest.json manifest (version from blend/VERSION; bump it first)
./blend/host/package.sh                          # -> blend/out/picori-seal-vX.Y.zip
```

## Code map

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `tmc/src/blend.c`, `tmc/include/blend.h` | Dark World palettes, Magic Mirror, Moon Pearl and bunny, rods, medallions, shared-slot items, item placement, co-op state sync, partner Link object |
| `tmc/src/objectUtils.c`, `tmc/src/scroll.c`, `tmc/src/ui.c` | Borrowed pictures for A Link to the Past items on the ground, held up and on the HUD |
| `tmc/src/fade.c` | Applies the Dark World palette as palettes are uploaded |
| `tmc/src/game.c` | Calls the blend update every gameplay frame |
| `blend/dungeons/` | Rebuilds A Link to the Past dungeons: `make-dungeons.sh` runs zelda3's room loader over your ROM, `build.py` turns each room into a Minish Cap room (`autotile.py` picks Minish Cap tile types, so collision works the Minish Cap way) and `tileset.py` draws the blended tileset from A Link to the Past's room graphics (`alttpgfx.py`) recolored by `blendstyle.py` and cel-shaded with soft shadows by `celshade.py`; `mcrender.py` renders rooms offline. Output goes to `tmc/custom/gen/` |
| `blend/anim/` | Link's animations for the A Link to the Past items: `build.py` draws A Link to the Past's item art, recolored to Link's Minish Cap palette, into Minish Cap Link poses (Hammer lifted and slammed, rods and Somaria swung, Hookshot held out, Byrna raised, Powder shaken, medallions and Mirror held up, Bug Net swung, Shovel driven down, Cape swept round; those last three are drawn by hand) as sprite 159; `objlists.py` splices generated frame object lists into `gFrameObjLists`; `mcsprite.py` reads and draws Minish Cap sprites. Run by `make-dungeons.sh`; output goes to `tmc/custom/gen/` |
| `blend/menu/icons.py` | The A Link to the Past item icons for the pause menu, the HUD and items on the ground: A Link to the Past's own item pictures, cut out, outlined and recolored to the Minish Cap item palettes, as frames 162 to 179 of sprite 322 (frames only the other languages' button words use; a keyframe can't name a frame past 255). Run by `make-dungeons.sh` |
| `blend/enemies/`, `tmc/src/enemy/alttpEnemies.c` | A Link to the Past's enemies: `alttp_enemies.py` cuts their sprites from your ALttP ROM, recolors them and adds new frames (as sprite 155); the C file gives each its behavior. Run by `make-dungeons.sh` |
| `blend/overworld/`, `tmc/src/blendShadows.c` | The blended overworld: `build.py` restyles every overworld palette toward A Link to the Past's Light World colors and draws soft shadow maps; `blendShadows.c` shows the shadows on background 3. Run by `build.sh`; output goes to `tmc/custom/gen/ow` |
| `blend/host/coop.c` | Split-screen co-op player (SDL2 + libmgba): state exchange, shared progress, quick inventory and prompts drawn over each player's screen, headless `--test` scripts |
| `tmc/src/blendCoop.c`, `tmc/include/blendCoop.h` | The game's side of co-op: going down and being revived, the Revive Fairy (sprite 338, item `ITEM_REVIVE_FAIRY`), the quick inventory's menu data and equip requests, items shared from the partner, warping to the partner, carrying and throwing the partner, giving hearts, and the co-op puzzles (pieces are `LINK_PARTNER` types 5 and 6; solved and taken bits in `gSave.filler3A[4]` and `[5]` (save bytes 0x3E and 0x3F)). Its block `gBlendCoop` sits at `0x0203F800` |
| `blend/coop/revive_fairy.py` | Draws the Revive Fairy's art (sprite and the host's copy). Run by `build.sh` |
| `blend/coop/twin_relics.py` | Draws the co-op puzzle pieces, the Twin Stone and the Toss Bell (sprite 339). Run by `build.sh` |
| `blend/host/updater.c` | The Windows player's auto updater: finds, checks (sha256, zip CRCs, ROM header), installs and restarts into newer builds |
| `blend/harness/harness.c` | Headless test runner used to check builds |

The shared state block `gBlendLink` sits at a fixed address, `0x0203F000`, so the
host can read each player's position and write the other player's in.
